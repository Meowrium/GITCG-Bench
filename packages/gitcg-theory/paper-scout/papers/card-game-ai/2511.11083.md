# Efficient Reinforcement Learning for Zero-Shot Coordination in Evolving Games (ScaPT)

- **arXiv ID**: [2511.11083](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11083)
- **Authors**: Bingyu Hui, Lebin Yu, Quanming Yao, Yunpeng Qu, Xudong Zhang, Jian Wang
- **Date**: 2025-11-14
- **Relevance**: ⭐⭐ (2/5) — LOW

## Summary

Population-based training for zero-shot coordination (ZSC) in Hanabi. Proposes a scalable meta-agent with mutual information regularizer for evolving partner pools. Focuses on coordination generalization — agents must cooperate with unseen partners from a diverse, evolving pool.

## Relevance to GI TCG

- ZSC is a multi-agent coordination problem, not a competitive game AI problem — not directly applicable to 1v1 TCG
- Hanabi is a cooperative game (players work together), fundamentally different from competitive GI TCG
- Mutual information regularization is a general technique but no clear application to our pipeline
- Archive only — no actionable items for GI TCG
