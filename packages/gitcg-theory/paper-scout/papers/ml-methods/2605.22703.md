# Clipping Bottleneck: Stabilizing RLVR via Stochastic Recovery of Near-Boundary Signals (NSR)

- **arXiv ID**: [2605.22703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22703)
- **Authors**: Shuo Yang, Jinda Lu, Chiyu Ma, Kexin Huang, Haoming Meng, Qihui Zhang, Yuyang Liu, Bolin Ding, Guoyin Wang, Li Yuan, Jingren Zhou
- **Date**: 2026-05-21
- **Relevance**: ⭐ (1/5) — LOW

## Summary

Identifies a bottleneck in GRPO-style RLVR training: hard clipping in PPO-like objectives discards informative gradient signals near the boundary. Proposes NSR (Stochastic Recovery of Near-Boundary Signals), which probabilistically recovers these signals instead of hard-clipping them. Improves training stability with minimal overhead.

## Relevance to GI TCG

- Pure RL optimization technique — no game-specific content
- Could benefit any GRPO/PPO-based training pipeline, but we don't use GRPO for GI TCG
