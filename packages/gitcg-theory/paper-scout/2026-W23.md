## HIGH Relevance — Deep Analysis

### 1. Self-Play RL for Big 2 (2605.28863) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

📄 [Full analysis](papers/card-game-ai/2605.28863.md) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28863)

**TL;DR**: First self-play RL framework for Big 2 (大老二), a 4-player imperfect-information card game. PPO dominates MC-Q, SARSA, and Q-learning under controlled comparisons. Entropy regularization prevents over-deterministic play; current-policy self-play is the strongest curriculum.

**Why it matters**: Big 2 is structurally the closest open testbed to GI TCG we've found — 4-player, imperfect information, variable action sets, sparse rewards. The PPO dominance finding validates our training approach. The entropy regularization insight is immediately actionable.

**Action**: Add entropy bonus to GI TCG PPO training. Evaluate Big 2 as intermediate testbed.

### 2. MAPLE: AlphaZero for IIG (2605.24139) ⭐⭐⭐⭐

📄 [Full analysis](papers/card-game-ai/2605.24139.md) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24139)

**TL;DR**: Bridges PIMC (strategy fusion) and IS-MCTS (expensive) by aggregating multi-state evaluations in a single search tree. Siamese-based sampling selects informative world states. +291 Elo on Phantom Go, +136 on Dark Hex.

**Why it matters**: Multi-state aggregation is the mechanism we need for MCTS under GI TCG's hidden opponent state. From Wu lab (Academia Sinica) — strong game AI group with Mahjong experience.

**Action**: Study MAPLE's aggregation mechanism for GI TCG MCTS. Adapt Siamese state sampling for opponent deck/hand hypotheses.

### 3. Schnapsen Shallow RL (2605.17162) ⭐⭐⭐⭐

📄 [Full analysis](papers/card-game-ai/2605.17162.md) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17162)

**TL;DR**: Progressive experimental study: imitation fails, RL succeeds. Key finding: learned value function + deeper lookahead search outperforms either alone. Shallow NN + async MC updates is sufficient.

**Why it matters**: Validates our MCTS+value architecture. Confirms imitation learning from game logs won't work for GI TCG. Progressive methodology is a model for our development process.

**Action**: Adopt progressive methodology. Verify "value + search > value-only" for GI TCG.

## Search Notes

### Methodology Evolution

This week exposed serious problems with the 26-query strategy from W22:

1. **Broad `all:` queries are useless**: 12 broad queries (T1 + first T2 query) returned the entire May 28 cs.AI daily listing — 47 papers, **0% card game relevant**. The cs.AI category filter adds almost no signal reduction for generic RL/game keywords.

2. **Rate limiting blocks consistency**: 14 of 26 queries hit 429 errors even at 3.5s delays. The arXiv API's official limit (1 req/3s) combined with broad queries means we can't reliably complete the full 26-query set.

3. **Smart queries work dramatically better**: 12 targeted title/phrase queries found 13 relevant papers (11% hit rate vs 0% for broad queries). Examples:
   - `ti:"card game"` → 0 results (genre terms not used in arXiv titles — try `ti:playing AND ti:card`)
