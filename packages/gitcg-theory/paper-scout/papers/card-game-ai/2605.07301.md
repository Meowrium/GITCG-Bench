# SOM: Structured Opponent Modeling for LLM-based Agents via Structural Causal Model

- **arXiv ID**: [2605.07301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07301)
- **Authors**: Shiyue Cao, Pei Xu, Likun Yang, Lei Cui, Xiaotang Chen, Kaiqi Huang
- **Date**: 2026-05-08
- **Relevance**: ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5) — MEDIUM

## Summary

Two-stage opponent modeling framework: (1) SCM construction captures directed links between opponent observations and actions, (2) LLM performs structured reasoning along SCM pathways. Outperforms SOTA LLM reasoning baselines on multi-agent benchmarks.

## Relevance to GI TCG

- SCM-based opponent modeling is methodologically clean and complementary to CGT
- Tested on general benchmarks, not card games specifically
- The SCM construction → structured reasoning pipeline could inform LLM deck prior design
- Note: SCM approach connects to MTG Causal RL paper (2605.06066) which also uses SCM
