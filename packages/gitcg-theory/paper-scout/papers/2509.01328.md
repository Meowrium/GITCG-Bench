## Summary

Explores whether LLMs can achieve success in complex games comparable to AlphaGo/AlphaZero/MuZero. Frameworks LLMs as game-playing agents and evaluates their strategic reasoning capabilities in card game domains. Tests LLMs on complex card games with imperfect information, multi-step planning, and opponent modeling — exactly the challenges in Genius Invokation TCG.

## Key Contributions

- First systematic evaluation of LLMs as agents in complex card games (not just text-based games)
- Benchmarks multiple LLMs on strategic decision-making under imperfect information
- Analyzes failure modes: planning horizon, state tracking, opponent modeling
- Proposes prompting strategies to improve LLM game performance
