# Optimizing Hearthstone Agents using an Evolutionary Algorithm

- **Authors**: Pablo García-Sánchez, Alberto Tonda, Antonio J. Fernández-Leiva, Carlos Cotta
- **arXiv**: [2410.19681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.19681)
- **Date**: 2024-10-25
- **Categories**: cs.NE
- **Citations**: N/A
- **Relevance**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

## Summary

Uses evolutionary algorithms (EAs) to develop Hearthstone agents by optimizing a data-driven decision-making mechanism. Addresses hidden information, uncertainty, and planning in collectible card games. Evolves agents that consider all game elements (hand, board, mana, opponent) for action selection.

## Key Contributions

- EA-based optimization of Hearthstone decision policies (alternative to RL/MCTS)
- Data-driven decision mechanism that handles the full game state
- Comparative analysis of evolved vs. handcrafted vs. random agents
- Demonstrates competitive performance without requiring massive RL training

## Connection to Our Work

- **Evolutionary approach** is interesting contrast to our GBDT ranker and RL training
- Hearthstone's mechanics (mana curve, minion combat, spell timing) partially map to GI TCG
- Data-driven decision mechanism could complement our GBDT ranker for policy initialization
- The EA approach could be used to evolve GI TCG deck lists or discover novel strategies
- Hidden information handling is directly applicable to GI TCG's fog-of-war

## Critical Assessment

**Strengths**: Novel EA approach for CCG AI (under-explored direction); handles full game state; cs.NE venue brings fresh perspective.  
**Weaknesses**: EA may not scale to GI TCG's combinatorial complexity; Hearthstone is simpler than GI TCG (no elemental reactions, 3-character teams); unclear if code released.
