# Cooperation and Exploitation in LLM Policy Synthesis for Sequential Social Dilemmas

- **arXiv ID**: [2603.19453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19453)
- **Authors**: Víctor Gallego
- **Date**: 2026-03-19
- **Relevance**: ⭐ (1/5) — LOW

## Summary

Uses LLMs to iteratively generate Python policy functions for multi-agent social dilemma games (e.g., Prisoner's Dilemma variants). Investigates feedback engineering: sparse vs. detailed performance feedback during refinement. LLM-generated policies evaluated in self-play and refined across iterations.

## Relevance to GI TCG

- Social dilemmas are cooperative/competitive hybrids — not 1v1 zero-sum card games
- The "LLM generates Python policy" pattern is tangentially related to Code World Models but at a much smaller scale
- Feedback engineering insights are too domain-specific to transfer
