# Suspicion-Agent: Playing Imperfect Information Games with Theory of Mind Aware GPT-4

- **arXiv ID**: [2309.17277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17277)
- **Authors**: Jiaxian Guo, Bo Yang, Paul Yoo, Bill Yuchen Lin, Yusuke Iwasawa, Yutaka Matsuo
- **Date**: 2023-09-29
- **Relevance**: ⭐⭐ (2/5) — LOW

## Summary

Introduces Suspicion-Agent, a framework using GPT-4 with theory-of-mind prompting for imperfect information card games. Tested on Leduc Hold'em (a minimal 6-card poker variant). Uses GPT-4's knowledge retrieval and reasoning to model opponent beliefs without explicit opponent modeling training.

## Relevance to GI TCG

- Leduc Hold'em is a toy game (6 cards, 2 actions) — results don't scale to GI TCG complexity
- Theory-of-mind prompting concept is interesting but the implementation is purely prompt-based (no training)
- Could inspire opponent modeling prompts for LLM-based GI TCG agents, but the gap from Leduc to GI TCG is enormous
- Archive — methodology too preliminary for practical use
