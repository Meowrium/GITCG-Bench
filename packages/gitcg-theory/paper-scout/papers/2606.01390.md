# Limit Continuous Poker: A Variant of Continuous Poker with Limited Bet Sizes
- arXiv ID: 2606.01390
- Authors: Andrew Spears
- Date: 2026-05-31
- Relevance Score: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Summary
Introduces Limit Continuous Poker, a simplified variant of Von Neumann's Continuous Poker with limited bet sizes. This variant captures core poker elements — information asymmetry, bluffing, balancing, bet size impact — while remaining analytically solvable. Derives full Nash equilibrium strategy profile and shows convergence to other poker variants at extreme bet limits. Connects theoretical results to strategic implications for real-world poker.

## Key Contributions
- **Analytically solvable poker variant**: Closed-form Nash equilibrium for a game capturing information asymmetry, bluffing, and bet size limits
- **Strategic analysis**: Shows how bet size limits shape optimal bluffing frequencies and calling thresholds
- **Limiting behavior**: Demonstrates convergence to known poker variants at extreme parameter values
- **Real-world poker connection**: Bridges analytical game theory to practical poker strategy

## Critical Assessment
**Strengths**: Clean mathematical analysis, tractable model that captures essential poker dynamics. Good pedagogical value. Connects theory to practice.

**Weaknesses**: Single-author, limited scope (two-player, one betting round). Not directly implementable for complex games like GI TCG. No computational experiments.

**Reproducibility**: High — mathematical derivations are self-contained.
