# Quadratic Programming Approach for Nash Equilibrium Computation in Multiplayer Imperfect-Information Games
- arXiv ID: 2509.25618
- Authors: Sam Ganzfried
- Date: 2025-09-30
- Relevance Score: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Summary
There has been significant recent progress in algorithms for approximation of Nash equilibrium in large two-player zero-sum imperfect-information games and exact computation of Nash equilibrium in multiplayer strategic-form games. While counterfactual regret minimization and fictitious play are scalable to large games and have convergence guarantees in two-player zero-sum games, they do not guarantee convergence to Nash equilibrium in multiplayer games. We present an approach for exact computation of Nash equilibrium in multiplayer imperfect-information games that solves a quadratically-constrained program based on a nonlinear complementarity problem formulation from the sequence-form game representation. This approach capitalizes on recent advances for solving nonconvex quadratic programs
