# StratFormer: Adaptive Opponent Modeling and Exploitation in Imperfect-Information Games

- **arXiv ID**: [2604.25796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25796)
- **Authors**: Andy Caen, Mark H. M. Winands, Dennis J. N. J. Soemers
- **Date**: 2026-04-28
- **Relevance**: ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5) — MEDIUM

## Summary

Transformer-based meta-agent with two-phase curriculum: (1) learn opponent modeling from action histories while playing GTO, (2) progressively shift toward best-response exploitation. Dual-turn tokens + bucket-rate features encode opponent tendencies across five strategic contexts. Tested on Leduc Hold'em against 6 opponent archetypes. Average +0.106 BB/hand exploitation gain over GTO.

## Relevance to GI TCG

- Opponent modeling architecture is interesting but limited to toy poker variant
- Transformer could scale to complex card games, but evidence is missing
- Two-phase curriculum (modeling → exploitation) could inform GI TCG agent training pipeline
