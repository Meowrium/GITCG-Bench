## Methodology Notes for Improvement

1. **Dedup fix needed:** seen.txt stores base arXiv IDs without version suffixes, but the API returns versioned IDs (e.g., `2606.13669v1`). Future runs must strip `v\d+$` before comparing. (Fixed in this week's execute_code.)
2. **Sort strategy:** `sortBy=submittedDate&sortOrder=descending` on broad `all:` queries produces ~90% noise. Consider `sortBy=relevance` for Tier 1, or switch to `ti:` prefix for precision queries.
3. **Semantic Scholar rate limit:** Batch queries with ≥3s delays to stay under 1 req/s. The execute_code pattern with 6 rapid calls in 9s triggered extended rate limiting.
